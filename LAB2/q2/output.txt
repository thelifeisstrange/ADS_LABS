============================================= MATHEMATICAL ANALYSIS =============================================
Total Operations (n)                : 16
Total Actual Cost (Sum c_i)         : 31
Total Amortized Cost (Sum c^_i)     : 32
Remaining Credit in Bank            : 1

THEORETICAL VERIFICATION:
1. Validity Condition: Sum(c^_i) >= Sum(c_i) for all n.
   - Here: 32 >= 31 (Credit Bank is always non-negative: 1 >= 0).
2. Amortized Cost Per Operation:
   - Every increment sets at most ONE '0' to '1' -> Allocated charge = 2 units.
   - Resetting any number of '1's to '0's consumes stored credit (Amortized charge = 0).
   - Therefore, Amortized Cost per increment = O(1) [constant = 2].
